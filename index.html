Loro

Offline flashcards that know when to ask again.

Loro is a quiet flashcard app for learning a language. It works entirely on your device — no account, no ads, no tracking, and no internet permission.

What it does

  • Drill a set of about twenty cards with two buttons, until you know them all.
  • Then let FSRS-6 spaced repetition decide when each card comes back.
  • Four language pairs, each keeping its own separate progress.
  • Interface in English, Polish, Spanish and Portuguese.

Your data

Everything Loro stores stays on your device. Nothing is collected, nothing is transmitted, and there is nowhere for it to go — the app cannot make network requests.
