Privacy Policy for Loro

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Loro is published by SkyBite sp. z o.o., a company registered in Poland.

The short version

Loro does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

What Loro stores on your device

Loro keeps two things locally, in the app's private storage:

  • Your study progress — which flashcards you have worked through, how you rated them, and when each one is next due for review.
  • Your settings — your chosen theme, study direction, session size, and scheduling preferences.

This information never leaves your device unless you deliberately export it yourself using the Export Backup feature, which hands a file to your device's share sheet so you can save or send it wherever you choose. Loro does not send it anywhere on its own.

What Loro does not do

  • No accounts. There is no sign-in, and no user identity of any kind.
  • No network access. The app does not request the Internet permission and cannot make network requests. Its flashcard content ships inside the app.
  • No analytics or tracking. No usage statistics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no cookies, no third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No advertising. The app contains no ads.
  • No data sharing. Since no data is collected, none is shared or sold.

Permissions

Loro requests no Android runtime permissions.

Children's privacy

Loro is safe for users of all ages. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it collects nothing from children either.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Loro removes all of its data from your device. You can also clear it at any time through Android's Settings → Apps → Loro → Storage → Clear storage.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a revised date above.

Your rights under the GDPR

SkyBite sp. z o.o. does not collect or process any personal data through Loro, so there is no personal data to access, correct, export or erase, and no data controller relationship arises from your use of the app. The study progress on your device is yours alone; you can export it from within the app and remove it by uninstalling.
